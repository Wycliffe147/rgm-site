Who We Are

Carrying the gospel to where the church hasn't yet reached.

Revealed Gospel Ministries is an evangelistical movement empowered to preach the gospel of salvation and reach out to the lost souls across the nations. We are based in Mzuzu, Malawi.

Every crusade is planned in close partnership with the local churches of that area, so the harvest is gathered into a community that will keep watering it. After each crusade, our partner churches lead the follow up and discipleship, with support from Life Ministries Malawi.

Founder

Apostle Mac Kawonga

Founder, RGM. Clinical Officer, Wezi Clinic

Apostle Mac Kawonga founded Revealed Gospel Ministries out of a burden to see the gospel reach unreached communities across Malawi. He currently serves as a clinical officer at Wezi Clinic while leading RGM's evangelistic work.

Faith in Action

RGM Charity Works

At RGM, we believe that after reaching people spiritually, we must also respond to their physical, emotional, and social needs. The love of Christ is demonstrated not only through preaching the Gospel but also through practical acts of compassion.

Since 2025, our dedicated Charity Works Department has supported vulnerable individuals and families with food, clothing, school fees, and other essential assistance, meeting needs in the communities where we've held crusades and beyond.
