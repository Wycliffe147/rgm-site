RGM Charity Works

At RGM, we believe that after reaching people spiritually, we must also respond to their physical, emotional, and social needs. The love of Christ is demonstrated not only through preaching the Gospel but also through practical acts of compassion.

Since 2025, we have operated a dedicated Charity Works Department that supports vulnerable individuals and families by providing food, clothing, school fees, and other essential assistance. This holistic approach has enabled us to meet the diverse needs of communities where we have conducted crusades and beyond, demonstrating the genuine love of God in action.
